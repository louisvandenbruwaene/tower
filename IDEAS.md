# Tower Power: ideas for later

The game is deliberately very simplified: three levels (low / mid / high), eight directions, one runway, every plane the same. These would make it more realistic, one at a time:

- **Real heights**: feet or flight levels instead of low / mid / high, with a minimum separation of 1000 ft.
- **Aircraft types**: small props, jets, heavies; different speeds, climb rates and turn rates; wake turbulence (more spacing behind a heavy).
- **Realistic distances**: nautical miles on the radar, separation of 3 to 5 nm, speeds in knots.
- **Wind**: decides the runway direction (take off and land into the wind), crosswind limits, wind drift in the air.
- **Weather**: storm cells to steer around, low visibility that needs longer final approaches.
- **Schedules**: a timetable of arrivals and departures, delays that build up, rewards for on-time handling.
- **More runways**: parallel or crossing runways, runway crossings by taxiing planes.
- **Handoffs**: neighbouring sectors that only accept planes at a given level and direction.
- **Emergencies**: low fuel, a medical case, a go-around with priority.
- **Radio phrasing**: optional real ATC phrases ("heron, descend and maintain four thousand").

## Roguelike progression

Each shift ends when you lose (too many strikes). You keep the tokens earned during it and spend them on permanent upgrades before the next shift:

- **More height levels**: a fourth and fifth level, so there is more room to stack planes apart and fewer crashes.
- **More runways**: a second runway (parallel or crossing) with its own landing circles.
- **Navigation system**: departures turn toward their destination by themselves after takeoff, and arrivals turn toward the nearest landing circle by themselves.
- **etc.**: other stats bought the same way.
