Tower Power

You are the air traffic controller. Every command has the same shape:

plane  action  [direction]   the direction is only needed for some actions.

heron turn sturn until it flies that way: n ne e se s sw w nw
heron turn lturn 90° left · sl sr = 45° · l r = 90° · hl hr = 135°
heron descenddown to low · heron descend mid stops at mid
heron ascendup to high · heron ascend mid stops at mid
heron holdcircle on the spot until you give it a turn, or land inside a landing circle · heron hold kilo flies to the fix KILO (also ALPHA, BRAVO, ZULU) and circles there
heron landonly inside an open landing circle (also ends a hold). It turns toward the runway by itself, even if that swings it a little outside, and lands once it faces it, low, with the runway free. Still high when it leaves the circle: it goes around.
wren takeoff edepartures in the ready list: it taxis from the apron to that runway end, waits there until the runway is free, takes off east or west, climbs to mid and flies straight on until you turn it (you may turn it while it taxis).
slow · fast · abortchange speed · cancel a hold (flies straight on) or a landing (also climbs to mid)
blue planesArrivals. They come in high. Bring them down to low, clear them to land and send them into one of the two landing circles at the runway ends.
gold planesDepartures. Each wants to leave in one of 8 directions (the gold slice of the circle). Take it off the runway way that points closest, then turn it that way before it reaches the edge of the circle: once it leaves, it is too late.
strikesTwo planes on the same level too close (red ring), a departure leaving through the wrong slice, or an arrival leaving your circle. Different levels cross safely. Three strikes and you replay the level.
purple planesOverflights from the neighbouring sector (in a shift, after 10 planes). They fly straight across: keep them apart from the rest and let them leave through their slice.
things take timeChanging level takes a while (bar under the label). Turning is gradual (dashed line = where it is turning to). The runway takes one plane at a time, until a landing plane has rolled out (amber while busy).
typesprop slow, turns tight · jet · bizjet fast, climbs quickly · heavy big, turns wide, climbs slowly, needs more room around it, and its runway rests a few seconds after it (wake)
fuelFrom level 5, arrivals carry fuel. Below 90 s it shows on the label, below 45 s it calls MAYDAY: land it next. Empty: a strike.
windFrom level 6, planes land and take off into the wind (top left): the other circles close. Now and then it turns.
stormsFrom level 7, storm cells drift across with the wind. A plane that flies into one: a strike.
readbackFrom level 6, a pilot sometimes mishears: the reply shows what it understood (a turn 45° off, another level). Say it again.
score100 per plane, less a point for every second it took beyond par. After the levels: shift, which never ends. Three strikes end it and pay a token per 100 points, to buy upgrades for later shifts (more levels up, a second runway, navigation, bigger tanks). daily: today's shift, the same for everyone, no upgrades.

Faded text in the prompt is a suggestion: → takes all of it, Tab takes one word. Capitals don't matter. Typos are fixed for you (nt = north): the reply shows what was understood. The first levels are slow and suggest every move; later ones go faster and leave it to you. demo shows a good controller at work, Esc pauses, help shows this card.
