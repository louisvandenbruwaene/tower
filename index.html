Tower Power

You are the air traffic controller. Every command has the same shape:

plane  action  [direction]   the direction is only needed for some actions.

heron turn sturn until it flies that way: n ne e se s sw w nw
heron turn lturn 90° left · sl sr = 45° · l r = 90° · hl hr = 135°
heron descenddown to low · heron descend mid stops at mid
heron ascendup to high · heron ascend mid stops at mid
heron holdcircle on the spot until you give it a turn, or land inside a landing circle · heron hold kilo flies to the fix KILO (also ALPHA, BRAVO, ZULU) and circles it, right turns, slowed down. Holds always stay inside your area
heron landonly inside an open landing circle (also ends a hold). Above low it circles there until you descend it; at low it lines up by itself and lands, arriving at most 15° off the runway heading. With the runway busy it circles until it is free (four times round at most, then it goes around)
wren takeoff ea departure in the ready list taxis to that runway end, waits until the runway is free, takes off that way, climbs to mid and flies straight on until you turn it (you may turn it while it taxis). The guide on the right lists the ways your runways take off
heron abortends a hold (straight on) or a landing: it goes around, climbing to mid
tutorial · speeda guided first landing, departure and hold (skip leaves it) · time 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× (both also buttons top right)

Faded text in the prompt is a suggestion: → takes all of it, Tab takes one word. Capitals don't matter. Typos are fixed for you (nt = north): the reply shows what was understood. Esc pauses, help shows this card.

Traffic

blue planesArrivals. They come in high: bring them down to low, into a landing circle, and clear them to land.
gold planesDepartures. Each wants to leave through one of 8 slices of your area (the gold one). Take it off the runway way closest to it, then turn it that way before it reaches the edge.
purple planesOverflights from the neighbouring sector, after 10 planes. They cross in a straight line: keep them apart and let them leave through their slice.
typesprop slow, turns tight · jet · bizjet fast, climbs quickly · heavy and cargo big, turn wide, climb slowly, need more room, and the runway rests after them (wake) · MIL fighters, fast and tight-turning
priorityVIP private jets (gold star) and MEDEVAC air ambulances (red cross) go first both ways: an arrival lands within 2 minutes, a departure is rolling within 50 s (the clock by its name), or it is a strike. While one waits, other planes of its kind may not land or take off (a MAYDAY may).
strikesTwo planes on the same level too close (red ring), a departure leaving through the wrong slice, an arrival leaving your area, a flight cancelled or late, fuel run out, a storm flown into. Touching shapes collide: two strikes. Different levels cross safely. Three strikes end the shift.
timeLevel changes take a while (bar under the label), turns are gradual (dashed line), the runway takes one plane at a time (amber while busy). 1 px is 25 m and time runs 4.5× fast; every type flies its own real speeds and turn rate.
fuelArrivals carry fuel (the jerrycan by the name): below 45 s they call MAYDAY, land them next. Departures have patience (the clock): when it runs out the flight is cancelled.
wind · stormsPlanes take off and land into the wind (top left): landing circles with the wind behind them close, a runway across the wind stays open. Storm cells drift with the wind and close the circles they cover.
readbackA pilot sometimes mishears: the reply shows what it understood. Say it again.

Money

moneyEvery plane pays its fare: prop $400, jet $1,000, heavy $1,600, fighter $1,500, private jet $4,000, air ambulance $3,500. It loses 1% per second beyond par (down to 10%); late priority flights pay a quarter. A streak (planes in a row without a strike) adds 10% a plane, up to +50%.
shiftOne shift that never ends: the first five planes in slow motion, the traffic growing for 15 minutes. It pays a token per $1,000 when it ends.
buyUpgrades for every later shift, one step at a time: tanks +20 s fuel, height levels top and peak, nav departures turn by themselves then land anywhere, runway less wake rest (10, 20, 50 … tokens), runways a parallel one (50), then crossing ones N-S (100), NE-SW (500), NW-SE (2000). Crossing runways share the centre (one busy, all busy) but open new circles and takeoff ways.
daily · harddaily: today's shift, the same for everyone, no upgrades. hard (after a $10,000 shift): no autopilot; land only once you have flown the plane onto the lit strip yourself, low and facing the runway, and hold alpha only at ALPHA.
