Tower

You are the air traffic controller. Every plane has a name. Type the name, then one or more orders.

blue planesArrivals. They come in high. Bring them down to low, get them inside the dashed ring around the runway, then type land. They line up and touch down by themselves.
gold planesDepartures. They wait in the ready list at the top. Type takeoff, climb them high and send them to the edge point (▲) named on their label. They leave when they reach it at H.
L · M · HThe level each plane flies at: low, mid, high. An arrow ↑↓ means it is still climbing or descending.
red ringTwo planes on the same level are too close: strike. Planes on different levels can cross safely. A plane flying off the screen is also a strike. Three strikes end the shift.
north … northwestturn to a compass direction (north, northeast, east, …)
left · rightturn an eighth of a circle
to maplefly straight to an edge point
high · mid · lowchange level
land · takeoffarrivals (low, inside the ring) · departures (ready list)
hold · slow · fastcircle on the spot · change speed
abortcancel a landing and climb to mid

Example: heron west low. The first letters are enough (he w lo). Tab or → completes a word. The green line above the prompt says what the plane you are typing needs next. ↑ repeats an earlier line, Esc pauses, help shows this card.
