Tower Power

You are the air traffic controller. Every command has the same shape:

plane  action  [direction]   the direction is only needed for some actions.

heron turn southturn until it flies south (north, northeast, east … northwest)
heron turn softleftturn 45° left · hardleft = 90° · softright · hardright
heron turn maplefly straight to an edge point ▲
heron descend lowchange level: climb / descend, to low · mid · high (no word = one level)
heron holdcircle on the spot until you give it a new turn
heron landarrivals that are low and inside the dashed ring; the plane lines up by itself
wren takeoffdepartures in the ready list; add a direction to send it somewhere else (wren takeoff north)
slow · fast · abortchange speed · cancel a landing
blue planesArrivals. They come in high. Bring them down to low, inside the ring, then land them.
gold planesDepartures. After takeoff they climb to high and fly to their exit by themselves. They leave when they reach it at H.
strikesTwo planes on the same level too close (red ring), or a plane flying off the screen. Different levels cross safely. Three strikes and you replay the level.
things take timeChanging level takes a while (bar under the label). Turning is gradual (dashed line = where it is turning to). The runway takes one plane at a time (it turns amber while busy).

Faded text in the prompt is a suggestion: → takes all of it, Tab takes one word. Capitals don't matter. A typo gets a "did you mean …?": answer yes or no. The first levels are slow and suggest every move; later ones go faster and leave it to you. demo shows a good controller at work, Esc pauses, help shows this card.
